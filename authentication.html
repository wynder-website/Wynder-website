Trust isn’t a feature.
It’s the entire product.

33% of UK buyers have unknowingly bought a counterfeit item, and only 5% of pre-owned watch purchases go through a specialist retailer.1 Every watch on Wynder is independently authenticated before it’s ever listed — here is exactly how.

The four-point process

  • 01

    Movement inspection

    The case is opened and the calibre verified against manufacturer specification by an in-house watchmaker, checking finishing, serial consistency and service condition.

  • 02

    Case, dial & bracelet check

    Every component is cross-referenced for period-correct parts, correct hallmarks and any sign of undisclosed replacement or refinishing.

  • 03

    Provenance & documentation

    Papers, warranty cards and service history are verified wherever available, and cross-checked against manufacturer production records.

  • 04

    Certified & sealed

    Once a watch passes every check, it is issued a Wynder certificate and sealed for insured transport — only then does it appear for sale.
