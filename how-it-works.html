Search. Verify. Wear.

No showroom visit, no guesswork. Here is exactly what happens between finding a watch and wearing it.

01

Search a curated selection

Filter by brand, reference number, box & papers, case size or budget. Every listing shows full condition photography, service history and provenance notes before you commit to anything.

  • Live inventory, updated as pieces are authenticated and listed
  • Instant financing quote shown against every watch
  • Save searches and get notified when a reference becomes available
02

We verify it, not you

Every watch that reaches the platform has already passed through our in-house authentication lab. Movement, case, dial, bracelet and documentation are each checked by a trained specialist before a listing goes live — see the full process on our Authentication page.

  • Movement opened and verified against manufacturer specification
  • Case, dial and bracelet cross-checked for period-correct parts
  • Every verified watch is issued a Wynder certificate
03

Choose how you pay

Buy outright, spread the cost with instant financing, or part-exchange a watch you already own — the value is applied automatically at checkout.

  • Decision on financing in minutes, entirely online
  • Instant part-exchange valuation on your current watch
  • 7-day money-back guarantee from the day it arrives
04

Insured delivery or collection

Every order ships in discreet, insured packaging via a dedicated courier network built for high-value goods — or collect in person from a Wynder showroom.

  • Insured delivery or collection in as little as 48 hours
  • Signature and ID verification required on delivery
  • Full tracking from dispatch to doorstep
05

Own it, wear it, trade it

Every watch includes a free warranty and access to our servicing network. When you're ready for something new, part-exchange or sell it back — or move to Wynder Access and rotate instead of own.

  • Warranty and servicing included on every purchase
  • Priority part-exchange pricing for returning customers
  • Trade in or move to a subscription at any time
