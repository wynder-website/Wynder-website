ID requirements for buying gold & silver.

Buying precious metals comes with checks that don’t apply to everyday purchases. This guide explains how much you can buy, when we ask for identification, and which documents we accept.

Purchase limits

How much you can buy

Online

Payment methodLimit per order
Debit or credit cardUp to £10,000
Bank transferUp to £20,000

Every customer account starts with a £10,000 transaction threshold. Providing two forms of ID lifts it, after which you can place unlimited orders of up to £20,000 each online.

In store (face to face)

Payment methodLimit
CashUp to £7,500 in any rolling year, counted from the date of your first cash purchase (GBP only)
Debit card (chip & PIN)No limit — UK-authorised cards, GBP only. We don’t accept credit cards in store.
Bank transferNo limit — UK bank, GBP only

ID is required for in-store purchases — see when we ask for ID below.

Why we have limits

The reason behind the checks

As a business that sells precious metals, we are required to follow UK rules on anti-money laundering, fraud and terrorist financing. That means putting checks and systems in place to help prevent financial crime, and taking proof of identity when certain transactions take place.

The Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017 require a business that deals in goods to carry out customer due diligence where it receives cash payments of £10,000 or more, whether in a single payment or in linked payments. Our own limits and ID thresholds on this page are how we apply those rules in practice.

For more on anti-money laundering rules for businesses like ours, see the government guidance linked at the bottom of this page.

When we ask for ID

Which purchases need identification

These requirements apply to personal buyers. If you are buying through a company, different requirements apply — please contact us before placing an order.

Online

Your purchaseWhat we ask for
A single order of £5,000 or moreTwo forms of ID
Several orders totalling up to £10,000Two forms of ID
Anything over £10,000Two forms of ID, to lift the restriction on your account

Every online payment also goes through fraud screening by our secure payment gateway. If the gateway recommends it, we may ask you for ID to confirm who you are and your address, even where the amounts above aren’t reached. We treat orders placed within a 48-hour period as multiple orders, and where several are placed from the same address we may contact you for identification.

In store (face to face)

Your purchaseWhat we ask for
Up to £5,000One form of photographic ID
Over £5,000Two forms of ID

These in-store limits apply to buying or selling, whether you pay by debit card, bank transfer or cash.

Accepted documents

What counts as ID

Photographic ID

  • Passport
  • Full UK driving licence

Proof of address

  • Bank statement, dated within the last 6 months
  • Utility bill, dated within the last 6 months
  • Council tax statement
  • Full UK driving licence (only if you haven’t already used it as your photo ID)

Not accepted

  • Credit card statements
  • Mobile phone bills

Your information

What happens to the ID you give us

We use the identification you provide to verify who you are, in line with anti-money laundering guidelines. It is not sold and it is not used for marketing. The details are recorded by our trained staff, solely in the course of their duties. For more on how we keep and use your data, please see our Privacy Policy.
